# QUANTEDGE

## Intelligent Portfolio Management, Forecasting, Optimization and Automated Paper Trading Platform

# Project Objective

Build a complete institutional-style quantitative investment platform from scratch.

This is not a stock prediction website.

This is a full-stack quantitative research, portfolio management, risk analytics, forecasting, optimization, explainability, and automated paper trading platform.

The application should resemble a combination of:

* Zerodha Kite
* TradingView
* Groww
* Bloomberg Terminal (simplified)
* QuantConnect (simplified)

The final product should be visually impressive, technically sound, production-ready, and suitable for a final-year engineering project.
